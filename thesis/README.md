# Dissertation outputs

## Figures

- `figure1_standardized_risk_measures`: monthly standardized VIX, published Cboe SKEW, reconstructed SKEW− and the 25D differential.
- `figure2_bt_horizon_coefficients`: B&T-style SKEW− coefficients and conventional 95% confidence intervals.
- `figure3_oos_forecast_performance`: recursive DD21-event Brier and ROC-AUC comparisons.

Captions and source lines are in `figures/figure_notes.md`.

## Tables

- `bt_replication.csv`: standalone B&T-style horizon estimates.
- `main_tailrisk_results.csv`: principal VIX-incremental and alternative-outcome results.
- `state_robustness_appendix.csv`: rolling and state-dependent specifications.
- `oos_results.csv`: dissertation-facing recursive forecast comparison.

The underlying licensed market and option datasets are not distributed. Data provenance is documented in the repository README.
