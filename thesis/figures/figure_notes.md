# Dissertation figure notes

## Figure 1. Standardized monthly option-implied risk measures

**Notes:** Each series is standardized over the full plotted sample for comparability. SKEW− is the put-only BKM measure reconstructed from OptionMetrics; 25D skew is the −25-delta put IV minus +25-delta call IV.

**Source:** OptionMetrics, Bloomberg/Cboe and author's calculations.

The supplied PNG and PDF use the final monthly series.

## Figure 2. SKEW− coefficients across downturn horizons

**Notes:** Points show coefficients from the B&T-style logit regressions of the monthly −5% downturn indicator on lagged SKEW− and the corresponding lagged downturn indicator. Error bars show conventional 95% confidence intervals.

**Source:** Author's calculations using OptionMetrics and S&P 500 market data.

## Figure 3. Recursive out-of-sample DD21-event forecasting performance

**Notes:** Forecasts are generated recursively using expanding estimation windows. Lower Brier scores indicate more accurate probability forecasts; higher AUC indicates better discrimination. Differences are descriptive because no formal paired forecast-comparison test is performed.

**Source:** Author's calculations.

The 2018–2025 period is a post-paper extension and is not presented as a pristine holdout.
